### SRTM Digital Elevation Model (30m)
- **What it contains:** Global terrain elevation at ~30m resolution
- **Source URL:** https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/ or via Google Earth Engine: `ee.Image("USGS/SRTMGL1_003")`
- **Access method:** Earth Engine Python API or manual download from EarthExplorer (free account required)
- **Spatial resolution:** ~30m (1 arc-second)
- **Temporal coverage:** Feb 2000 (single acquisition)
- **Projection:** WGS84 (EPSG:4326); reproject to UTM 43N before use
- **Licence:** Public domain (NASA/USGS)
- **Downloaded:** —
- **Stored at:** `data/raw/dem_srtm_30m.tif` (NOT committed)
- **Notes / Limitations:** SRTM has voids in steep terrain; use the "filled" version. For Karnataka plains, quality is generally good.

- **What it contains:** National land use classification for India
- **Source URL:** https://bhuvan.nrsc.gov.in/bhuvan_links.php (LULC 2011-12 or 2015-16)
- **Access method:** Manual download after free registration on Bhuvan portal
- **Spatial resolution:** 1:50,000 (vector) or 30m (raster product)
- **Temporal coverage:** 2011-12 or 2015-16
- **Projection:** WGS84 or local; check header
- **Licence:** NRSC data — free for non-commercial research with attribution
- **Downloaded:** —
- **Stored at:** `data/raw/lulc_bhuvan.tif` (NOT committed)
- **Notes / Limitations:** Registration required. May take 24–48 hours for access approval. The 56-class system is very detailed; you will reclassify to a simplified scheme for the model.

- **Spatial resolution:** ~5km (0.05°)

- **Licence:** Public domain
- **Downloaded:** —
- **Stored at:** `data/raw/rainfall_chirps_annual_mean.tif` (NOT committed)
- **Notes / Limitations:** Coarser than DEM; bilinear resampling needed to match 30m grid.
